01 Who we are

We are a compact mobile game studio building prototypes across hyper, hybrid, and casual genres — from fast HC loops to hybrid casual with meta-game depth and full casual production with progression and IAP economy. Our preferred format is a 4-week sprint — delivering a fully polished, pro-level prototype with deep mechanics, refined balance, and production-grade UA creatives. We ship on both Android and iOS, and can publish on our own Google Play and App Store developer accounts. We use AI to multiply creative and balance iterations — variants are generated fast, then benchmarked against human-made baselines before anything ships. Our founder has first-hand CrazyLabs pipeline experience.

02 How we work

Focused sprints with a clear deliverable at the end. Every prototype ships complete — build, SDK, and creatives included. Flexible engagement: work-for-hire, rev-share, or PPP (Pay-per-prototype) — PPP is our preferred structure, but we adapt to what fits your pipeline.

Preferred format

4 wk

Pro-level, ship-ready prototype

  • Deep mechanics & refined game design
  • Balance, progression & juice pass
  • Full monetization: analytics + ads SDK
  • 5–6 production-grade UA creatives
  • Dual-platform build: Android + iOS
  • Publishable on our own store accounts

Also available

  • Android-first playable build
  • 2–3 UA hook creatives (test batch)

  • No full monetization or balance pass

03 20 working days

How a 4-week sprint breaks down — enforced by hard freezes, so week 4 stays reserved for release, not finishing.

Week 01 · Foundation

Concept & core loop

  • Brainstorm, dev-plan, task alignment
  • Core mechanic prototyped
  • Moodboard & early assets
  • Style freeze · day 5

Week 02 · Build phase

Content & systems

  • Final assets integrated in waves
  • Features, progression, tutorial
  • Ads SDK & analytics events wired
  • Feature freeze · day 10

Week 03 · Polish + UA

Balance & creatives

  • Juice, balance, bug pass
  • 5–6 UA creatives rendered & stitched
  • Store stills, gameplay capture
  • QA builds · day 15

Week 04 · Release

Buffer & publish

  • Test fixes (Mon–Wed)
  • Store listings & copy
  • Stores submit · day 18
  • Release · day 20

🔒 Hard checkpoints. Style freeze (day 5) and feature freeze (day 10) lock scope early. Week 4 is release logistics — not finishing work.
