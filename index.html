About Us

Independent mobile games studio focused on polished, retention-driven experiences

Our Mission

Taratorkin Official is an independent mobile games studio with a clear focus: build polished, retention-driven experiences that players return to. Since 2020 our core team has been designing, prototyping and shipping titles across the hyper / hybrid-casual space.

We combine fast iteration with production discipline to deliver both hits and steady-grossing live games. Our approach blends creative design, lean engineering and data-informed live-ops.

We cover the full product lifecycle for mobile games: rapid prototyping, full production, soft-launch tuning and long-term live support. Our approach blends creative design, lean engineering and data-informed live-ops so prototypes scale cleanly into live products.

  • Genres: hyper / hybrid casual, puzzles, idle, arcade, runners, tower defense
  • Prototyping: rapid, playable prototypes that validate core loop
  • Production & polish: robust Unity-based pipelines, art direction and UX
  • Live-ops & support: A/B testing, event-driven content, retention & LTV optimization
  • Monetization: IAP economy design, rewarded ads and mediation
  • Cross-functional delivery: design, engineering, pixel art and animation
  • Full-cycle development: from concept and prototyping to full production, QA, soft-launch and live-ops
  • Experienced & focused: a compact team that moves fast without sacrificing discipline
  • Data-driven decisions: qualitative design intuition with quantitative metrics
  • Flexible engagement: we prototype, co-develop, or run full production
  • Player-first design: clear, rewarding core loops and fair monetization
  • Collaboration mindset: transparent roadmaps, clear milestones
  • End-to-end partner: from initial concept to market with live-ops support
  • Playable prototypes that prove mechanics and monetization hypotheses.
  • Full production-ready games with polished UX, art and sound.
  • Live-ops plans and ongoing content/analytics support to grow retention and revenue.
  • Consulting on economy tuning, ad strategy and A/B testing frameworks.
