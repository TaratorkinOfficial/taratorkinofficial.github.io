About the studio

Who we are.

We are a compact, founder-led mobile game studio specializing in rapid hyper-casual prototyping. Our preferred format is a 4-week sprint — delivering a fully polished, pro-level prototype with deep mechanics, refined balance, and strong UA potential. We actively use AI tools for content creation, game balance, and optimization — always with human analysis and review. Our founder has first-hand CrazyLabs pipeline experience.

How we work.

Focused sprints with a clear deliverable at the end. Every prototype ships complete — build, SDK, and creatives included.
