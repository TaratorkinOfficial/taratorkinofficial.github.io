About the studio

Who we are.

We are a compact mobile game studio specializing in rapid hyper-casual prototyping. Our preferred format is a 4-week sprint — delivering a fully polished, pro-level prototype with deep mechanics, refined balance, and production-grade UA creatives. We ship on both Android and iOS, and can publish on our own Google Play and App Store developer accounts. We use AI to multiply creative and balance iterations — variants are generated fast, then benchmarked against human-made baselines before anything ships. Our founder has first-hand CrazyLabs pipeline experience.

How we work.

Focused sprints with a clear deliverable at the end. Every prototype ships complete — build, SDK, and creatives included.

20 working days.

How a 4-week sprint breaks down — enforced by hard freezes, so week 4 stays reserved for release, not finishing.

Week 01 · Foundation

Concept & core loop

  • Brainstorm, dev-plan, task alignment
  • Core mechanic prototyped
  • Moodboard & early assets
  • Style freeze · day 5
Week 02 · Build phase

Content & systems

  • Final assets integrated in waves
  • Features, progression, tutorial
  • Ads SDK & analytics events wired
  • Feature freeze · day 10
Week 03 · Polish + UA

Balance & creatives

  • Juice, balance, bug pass
  • 5–6 UA creatives rendered & stitched
  • Store stills, gameplay capture
  • QA builds · day 15
Week 04 · Release

Buffer & publish

  • Test fixes (Mon–Wed)
  • Store listings & copy
  • Stores submit · day 18
  • Release · day 20
🔒 Hard checkpoints. Style freeze (day 5) and feature freeze (day 10) lock scope early. Week 4 is release logistics — not finishing work.
