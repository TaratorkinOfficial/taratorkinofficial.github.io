03 · Rules

Core rules

01

Art-style freeze

Friday of week 1 — visual direction is locked. No style re-works allowed after this point.

02

Feature freeze

Friday of week 2 — feature set is locked. Nothing new added, only fixes and polish from here.

03

Weekend iOS QA

The artist owns the only iPhone. Sat–Sun testing after day 15, no working days burned.

04

Week 4 is a buffer

Mon–Wed: fix what the tests surfaced. Thu: stores submit. Fri: release. Not a week for finishing work.

05

99% ready by week 4

We enter week 4 with a near-complete build. Unfinished features at this point mean the methodology has slipped.

06

15-minute standup

Morning sync, strict Trello discipline. The helper takes narrow, isolated tasks — working the afternoon shift.
