02 · Studio

Built like a bigger studio

Taratorkin Official is an independent mobile games studio with a clear focus: build polished, retention-driven experiences that players return to. Since 2020 our core team has been designing, prototyping and shipping titles across the hyper, hybrid, and casual space.

We combine fast iteration with production discipline to deliver both hits and steady-grossing live games. Our approach blends creative design, lean engineering and data-informed live-ops.

AI is part of our pipeline. We use it to multiply creative variants, accelerate balance passes, and rapidly iterate on UA hooks — but every AI-generated output is benchmarked against a human-made baseline before shipping. Speed never comes at the cost of quality, and a compact team operates at the throughput of a much larger studio.

From rapid prototyping to full production and long-term live-ops, we cover the entire lifecycle of a mobile game. Our toolbox spans A/B testing, event-driven content, retention & LTV optimization, IAP economy design, and rewarded-ad mediation — across hyper, hybrid, and casual genres, all under one roof.
